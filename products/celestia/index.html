The Scent Narrative

Celestia captures the silence of Indian botanical gardens in the hour before dawn, when night-blooming petals surrender their purest, dew-washed nectar.

The fragrance lifts with crisp Italian green bergamot and neroli blossoms before revealing its true sovereign: authentic Sambac jasmine hydro-distilled in Kannauj. Devoid of screechy indoles or sugary synthetic accents, the white petals rest upon an airy bed of cashmere woods, silky botanical pearl musk, and translucent solar amber that feels like warm sunlight on raw silk.

Botanical Provenance

The Midnight Harvest of Kannauj

Sambac jasmine flowers are notoriously ephemeral. The moment the sun rises over Uttar Pradesh, heat begins breaking down the most fragile aromatic esters.

Our partners hand-gather blossoms between 2:00 AM and 5:00 AM while the petals remain tightly swollen with nocturnal nectar. They are transferred immediately to water-chilled distillation tanks, preserving the sparkling green facet that defines Celestia.
