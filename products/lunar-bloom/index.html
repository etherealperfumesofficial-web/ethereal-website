The Scent Narrative

Lunar Bloom discards the polite, powdery rose of classic Western perfumery in favor of an opulent, nocturnal flower wrapped in temple smoke and ancient woods.

The scent opens with a sharp flash of pink peppercorn and sun-dried rosebuds before unfolding into a dense, honeyed core of hydro-distilled Damascena rose absolute. A subtle ribbon of frankincense smoke weaves through the floral heart, anchoring into aged Mysore sandalwood, dark Indonesian patchouli leaves, and warm black amber. It is mysterious, sensual, and thoroughly unhurried.

The Application Ritual

The Secret of Sandalwood & Rose

In traditional Indian perfumery, rose oil is distilled directly into a base of Mysore sandalwood oil through the ancient Deg-Bhapka process.

Because rose petals contain volatile top-tier aroma molecules that dissipate rapidly on their own, the heavy, oily molecules of aged sandalwood act as a natural lock. The result is a rose that breathes continuously for half a day, evolving from spicy freshness to deep, leathery petals.
