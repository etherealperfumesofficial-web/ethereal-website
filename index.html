The Atelier Philosophy

Where Indian botanical patience meets Middle-Eastern depth.

We do not believe in synthetic volatility or fleeting top notes. Our perfumes are matured in shaded glass vessels for months until botanical oils, precious balsams, and wild resins meld into a seamless second skin.

From dew-soaked jasmine harvested at midnight in Uttar Pradesh to aged Cambodian agarwood and Kashmiri saffron threads, every extract is sourced with reverence for the hands that cultivate it.

The Raw Botanicals

Sourced at Their Geographic Heart

We travel to indigenous soil where soil chemistry, altitude, and centuries of artisan harvesting yield absolute purity.

Cambodia & Mysore

Wild Oud & Sandalwood

Aged agarwood chips slow-distilled in copper stills, yielding a balsamic, leathered depth.

Kannauj, Uttar Pradesh

Sambac Jasmine

Plucked before daybreak to trap the intoxicating floral indole before sunlight evaporates the nectar.

Damascus Heritage

Damascena Rose

Hydro-distilled in small batches for honeyed spice and crimson velvety petals.

Pampore, Kashmir

Mongra Saffron

Hand-separated red stigmas that bring a bitter-sweet metallic glow to our amber accords.
